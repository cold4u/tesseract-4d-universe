💡 Terminology Clarification

Is it called a "4D Tesseract" or just "Tesseract"?
In pure mathematics, a cube is inherently 3-dimensional, and a Tesseract is by definition always 4-dimensional! Calling it a "4D Tesseract" is common in popular science, but scientifically saying "The Tesseract (4D Hypercube)" is the most precise and elegant title because it clarifies that the Tesseract is the 4-dimensional sibling of the 3D cube and the 2D square!

How to Understand the 4th Dimension

A square has 4 vertices. Move it in 3D to create a cube with 8 vertices. Move that cube into the 4th dimension (perpendicular to all 3 spatial directions) to create a Tesseract with 16 vertices, 32 edges, 24 square faces, and 8 cubic cells!
