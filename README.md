## 🌟 Observatories & Cosmic Stations

### 1. 🌌 Milky Way Galaxy 3D Simulator
- 20,000+ stars procedurally generated using Lindblad logarithmic spiral density wave physics ($r = a e^{b\theta}$).
- Volumetric interstellar dark dust lanes (*The Great Rift*) between spiral arms.
- Supermassive Black Hole **Sagittarius A*** ($4.1 \times 10^6 M_\odot$) glowing at the core with high-velocity orbiting stars.
- Interactive fly-to camera controls: **Our Solar System** (26,000 light-years out), Galactic Core, and Whole Galaxy overview.

### 2. 🕳️ Real Black Hole: Collision & Spaghettification Sandbox
- General relativistic 3D Black Hole featuring **Gargantua-style gravitational lensing** (accretion disk light bent up and over the top and bottom of the event horizon), relativistic Doppler beaming, and photon sphere caustic ring.
- **Interactive "What If?" Collision Sandbox**:
  - **Encounter the Sun**: Click **"▶ Play Simulation"** to watch the Sun travel along its orbital decay path, stretch past the Roche limit ($R_T$) into a teardrop shape, and violently shred into a 3,500-particle fluid plasma ribbon while relativistic jets erupt at $0.998c$!
  - **Encounter Earth**: Watch Earth's atmosphere get stripped into space and the planet torn by extreme tidal forces.
  - **Light Deflection**: See rays of background starlight bent into glowing Einstein rings.

### 3. 🌀 Traversable Wormhole (Einstein-Rosen Bridge) Flight Simulator
- **Fly directly inside and through a 4D curved spacetime throat!**
- Relativistic flight velocity slider: Adjust from $0.1c$ up to **$9.9c$ Maximum Warp**.
- Watch stars streak into relativistic warp lines, travel through the curved hyperspace throat, and emerge into a distant galaxy on the other side!

### 4. 💥 The Big Bang & Cosmic Web Origin Simulator
- **Detonate the Universe from $t = 0$!**
- Hit **"Ignite Big Bang"** to trigger a blinding Planck singularity ($10^{32} \text{ K}$) that detonates in a relativistic cosmic inflation shockwave.
- Watch **20,000 primordial plasma particles** expand, cool, and gravitationally condense before your eyes into the giant **Cosmic Web** (filaments of galaxies and cosmic voids)!
- Scrub through 13.8 Billion years of cosmic history with live physics telemetry.

### 5. ⚡ Pulsar (Neutron Star) Cosmic Lighthouse
- Rapidly spinning magnetized neutron star core with magnetic crust striations.
- Dual relativistic synchrotron radiation beams shooting along magnetic poles.
- Synchrotron beams sweep across your screen like a cosmic lighthouse beam with synchronized audio radio clicks.

### 6. 🚀 3D Multi-Stage Rocket: Exploded View
- Detailed 3D model of a deep space multi-stage rocket vehicle.
- **3D Explode Slider (0% to 100%)**: Smoothly pull apart the aerodynamic payload fairings, interplanetary probe, cryogenic upper stage, vacuum engine, interstage lattice, propellant tanks, and booster engine cluster!

### 7. 🛰️ Voyager 1 & 2 Interstellar Missions
- Live real-time distance ticker from Earth (in AU and billions of kilometers, ticking live by ~17 km every second!).
- One-way light travel time calculation.
- The Golden Record interactive showcase and historic missions compendium (Apollo 11, JWST, New Horizons).

### 8. 🧊 The Tesseract (4D Hypercube)
- One clean, standalone, fun interactive 3D model of the 4th-dimensional hypercube.
- Drag to orbit in 3D; slide the 4D Inversion control to watch the hypercube turn itself inside-out.
- Educational terminology callout explaining why "Tesseract" inherently means 4D.
